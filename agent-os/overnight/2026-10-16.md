- `94bbe23a` — **Tomboy import: emphasis around a space was not emphasis, and an impossible
  character reference failed the whole note.** Tomboy formats the space along with the word,
  so `<bold>word </bold>next` became `**word **next`, which CommonMark does not render as bold
  (a closing marker may not follow whitespace); the whitespace now sits outside the markers.
  `decodeXml` also passed any numeric reference to `String.fromCodePoint`, which throws past
  U+10FFFF and returned NUL for `&#0;` (a Postgres text column refuses it); such a reference
  stays as written, as `parseXml` does for Achieve since 10-12. — verified by two new cases in
  `markup.test.ts` (both failed first).
- `bb96ffa7` — **Page-title fetch decoded entities twice.** `decodeBasicEntities` chained one
  replace per entity, so `&amp;lt;` became `<` instead of the text `&lt;`; an out-of-range
  numeric reference threw and the catch in `fetchPageTitle` reported "no title" for the page.
  One pass, impossible references dropped. — verified by two new cases in `pageTitle.test.ts`
  (both failed first).
- `603fff2e` — **A daily appointment older than 500 days disappeared from the calendar, and
  a weekly one on several weekdays lost all but its first day each week once its tally passed 500.** `expandRecurrence` capped its loops on `index` — the occurrence's _position in the
  series_, which the daily/weekly fast-forward deliberately starts at the jump target (the
  end-after-N tally needs that) — so the cap meant "how old is the series", not "how much
  work did this call do". Daily: loop condition already false on entry for any series older
  than `maxOccurrences` (500) days. Weekly: the inner loop returned the moment `index` crossed
  500 (three weekdays: ~3 years), and `weekIndex < maxOccurrences * 2` killed the week loop
  past ~19 years. The most exposed rows are the oldest ones — imported Achieve routines; the
  dev DB has no recurring appointments, so I could not look at real ones. Caps now count
  steps walked, weeks walked and occurrences emitted. Monthly/yearly never fast-forward, so
  their cap is still a cap on the walk and is unchanged (≈41 years of monthly). — verified by
  six new cases in `recurrence.test.ts` (all fail before) and a scratch fuzz of 4,000 random
  daily/weekly series against an independent day-by-day generator: 740 mismatches before,
  0 after.
- `565a373f` — **The next-payday walk clamped to 300 cadences.** `walkForward` computes the
  step count by division and then `Math.min`'d it with 300 "so a corrupt anchor fails fast" —
  nothing loops. The clamp's only effect: a weekly override anchored more than ~5.75 years
  back answered a date in the past (2023-10-06 for a 2018 anchor) with a negative days-away on
  Available. Removed. — verified by a new `available.test.ts` case (failed first).
- `f75e8ed6` — **Achieve RTF notes: curly quotes became control characters, emoji and
  non-Latin text vanished, `\{ \}` lost their meaning, and group content leaked.**
  `rtfToPlainText` was a regex chain. `\'92` became U+0092 (invisible) instead of ’ — `\'hh`
  is Windows-1252, and RichEdit writes curly quotes that way; `\uN` with N < 0 was dropped
  though RTF writes UTF-16 units as signed 16-bit (every emoji and everything above U+7FFF);
  `\{` lost its brace but kept the backslash; `{\info …}`, pictures and field instructions
  one group deeper than the regex's `[^}]*` leaked words into the note; `\par` / `\line`
  matched the front of longer control words; raw CR/LF were kept as text. Rewritten as a
  single pass with a group stack, `\ucN`, and NUL stripping. **Notes already imported by the
  old reader are not repaired** — a re-import would be needed. — verified by nine new cases
  in `rtf.test.ts` (eight failed before) and a Delphi/RichEdit-shaped sample by hand.
