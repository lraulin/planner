[ ](screenshots19/WeeklyPlanningWizardDropBlocks.png) Weekly Planning Wizard Use the weekly planning wizard to plan out your week, or do it yourself directly into the weekly schedule. The wizard helps you decide what projects you want to focus on each week and how much time you want to commit to them. Then you can schedule project blocks for each of those projects in your schedule.
