# Achieve Time Management & Goal Setting Software Help - Uninstalling Achieve

_Archived from [http://www.effexis.com/achieve/online_documentation/uninstallingachieve.htm](http://www.effexis.com/achieve/online_documentation/uninstallingachieve.htm)_

---

**_To uninstall (remove) Achieve from your system:

· Open the "Add/Remove Programs" from the Control Panel
· Find the Achieve program
· Click the Remove button
\- or -
· For some systems, you may need to click on the Change/Modify button and then choose Remove from the installer options.
If you have any questions regarding uninstallation, please send an email to _support [at] effexis.com_
