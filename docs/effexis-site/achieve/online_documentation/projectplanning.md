# Achieve Time Management & Goal Setting Software Help - Project Planning

_Archived from [http://www.effexis.com/achieve/online_documentation/projectplanning.htm](http://www.effexis.com/achieve/online_documentation/projectplanning.htm)_

---

Achieve projects can vary greatly from simple single-step actions (e.g., take dog to vet) to large complex projects involving many steps (see [Getting Organized Overview](gettingorganizedoverview.md)). These different types of projects require different levels of planning and detail in order to be successful. In fact, small simple projects may not require any planning at all. The following guidelines are meant for the larger more complex projects that can truly benefit from careful deliberate planning. Use your judgement to decide what level of planning (if any) each of your projects requires.
Visit the _[Time Management Guide](http://www.timethoughts.com/timemanagement.htm)_ online for more information on project planning.
