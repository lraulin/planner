# Achieve Time Management & Goal Setting Software Help - Changing Appointments/Events

_Archived from [http://www.effexis.com/achieve/online_documentation/changingappointmentsevents.htm](http://www.effexis.com/achieve/online_documentation/changingappointmentsevents.htm)_

---

You can change an appointment or event through drag and drop or by opening the [Appointment Information form](appointmentinformationform.md).
You can move one or more appointments or events by dragging them to their new location using the mouse. To change the duration you can also resize appointments at their top/bottom edges (cursor will change to a resize cursor) and events at their left/right edges.
To change the Subject of an appointment or event, you can click on the subject and edit it in place or change it through the information form.
To obtain the information form for an appointment or event, you can double click it, press the _Enter_ key when selected, or invoke the **File Open Open Selected Item(s)...** command.
