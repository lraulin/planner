# Achieve Time Management & Goal Setting Software Help - Wish Brainstorming Wizard

_Archived from [http://www.effexis.com/achieve/online_documentation/wishbrainstormingwizard.htm](http://www.effexis.com/achieve/online_documentation/wishbrainstormingwizard.htm)_

---

The Wish Brainstorming Wizard consists of several steps to help you brainstorm and identify all your wishes (needs, desires, wants) for one or more result areas. You can invoke the wizard from the [Result Areas](resultareas.md) and [Wish List](wishlist.md) views directly using the [Tools menu](toolsmenu.md).
Wishes come in four types:
· Want and Don't Have (W/DH) - These represent a need/desire for something that you don't already have. This is the most common category.
· Don't Want and Have (DW/H) - These represent a desire to get rid of or change something that you have. It could be an extra 15 pounds or a dead end job.
· Want and Have (W/H) - These represent a need/desire to get more or enhance something you have.
· Don't Want and Don't Have (DW/DH) - These represent a desire to keep away or avoid a threat of some type.
Steps 1-4 Identify Wishes
Steps 1 through 4 walk you through a series of questions designed to help you brainstorm and identify all your needs, wants and desires in the selected result area. Each step is designed to elicit wishes of each type (Want and Don't Have, Don't Want and Have, etc.).
Just read the question and enter as many items as you can think of in the grid. Use the **Wizard Next Item** command to advance to the next question. Once the last question is reached the command will automatically take you to the next step.
Step 5 Priority Category
Step 5 is the first step in prioritizing wishes, in this step you are asked to assign one of the following priority categories to each wish:
A - Very important. A wish that would make a significant impact in your life and that you would like to pursue.
B - Important. A wish that would make a positive impact in your life but not as great as the A wishes.
C - Good. A wish that would make a difference in your life and that you may want to pursue at some future time.
D - Not Important. A wish that might make a difference in your life but that you are not willing to consider at this time.
You can review the priority values later using the [Wish List](wishlist.md) view (see [Using Priorities](usingpriorities.md) for more details.)
You can also adjust the Wish Type using this step.
Step 6 Priority Rank
In this step you are asked to assign a rank value A1, A2, A3... etc to the top five wishes. These constitute the wishes that would make the greatest impact in this result area and that you would like to start pursuing in the near future.
Step 7 Next
From this step you can exit the wizard or restart with a new result area.
