Executive Coaching Profile - Facebook's CEO An important aspect of [executive coaching](http://www.executivecoachinghq.com) is the idea of modeling excellence. Looking for positive role models, identifying their most positive and beneficial mindsets, strategies and tactics, and adapting them to your own situation and needs.
Facebook founder and CEO Mark Zuckerberg was recently named Time's "Person of the Year" for 2010, joining the rank of past winners that includes famous celebrities, inventors and heads of states as the person who the magazine believes most influenced events in the past year.
Time honored Zuckerberg for changing how we all live our lives. At 26, Zuckerberg is the second youngest person of the year selected by the magazine.
What could you learn from Mark Zuckerberg and apply to your own situation? How can your [ executive coach](http://www.executivecoachinghq.com/executive-coach) help you adapt your implementation, mindset and leadership style?
