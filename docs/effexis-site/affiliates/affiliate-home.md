**Step 2: Sign Up With 1ShoppingCart.com**
Use the link below signup for the Effexis Affiliate program administered through the 1ShoppingCart.com system.
[Click Here to Sign Up for the Effexis Affiliate Program](https://www.mcssl.com/SYS/?m=101523&c=s)
**Step 3: Get Your Links**
Once you've been approved into the program, please log in to the system to get your links. **Failing to do this means that 1ShoppingCart will be unable to track your referrals.**
[Click Here to Log In](https://www.mcssl.com/SYS/?m=101523&c=l)

Please use the appropriate link depending on the specific product that you want to promote. We recommend using the Achieve Planner Product Page link, although you might want to send leads directly to the download page link if you've presold them.
